Privacy Policy — Naya Sahar Dharmik Sikshya (New City Catechism)

Effective date: August 30, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Naya Sahar mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. Naya Sahar is a catechism study app that presents the New City Catechism questions and answers, commentary, and prayers, with optional daily reminders.

Short version: the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collection

Naya Sahar does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app settings — including language preference, theme (light/dark), children's mode, and notification preferences — are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

We do not:

  • collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal information;
  • create user accounts or require you to sign in;
  • send any of your data to us or to any external server.

Local Storage

The App saves your preferences and reading progress locally on your device (using your device's standard on-device storage) so that your settings and place in the catechism are remembered between sessions. This information is stored only on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Notifications

The App offers an optional daily notification that delivers a catechism question at a time you choose. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using local scheduling — no data is sent to any external server to deliver them. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in the App's settings, or through your device's system settings.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Your usage of the App remains completely private. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that could collect your data. There are no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or social sharing that receive your information.

Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages and includes an optional children's mode. Because the App collects no data whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) or from any other users. The App is compliant with children's privacy requirements by virtue of collecting no data.

Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data for us to secure on any server. Your preferences remain on your own device under the protection of your device's own security.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You can clear all locally stored settings at any time by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page and within the App, with an updated "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
